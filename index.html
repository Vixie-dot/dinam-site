Questions

Good to know

Does Dinam collect my data?

No. Dinam has no servers and no accounts. What you add is saved only on your phone. The privacy policy lists the two things that can use the internet, and only when you use them.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline. Turn on Offline mode and Dinam never touches the internet.

What if I change my phone?

Make a backup from Settings and restore it on the new phone. The backup file goes only where you choose to save it.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Dinam is Android only for now.

How much does it cost?

Dinam is free while it is being tested.
