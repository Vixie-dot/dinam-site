Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The short version. Dinam has no servers and no accounts. Everything you add is stored only on your phone. We cannot see your data, and we never collect, sell or share it.

Dinam is a free Android app made by Hiruthikvigasin, Tenkasi, India ("we"). You can reach us at support@dinam.co.in.

What stays on your phone

Your tasks and reminders, food, water and workout logs, money entries and budgets, medicines, period, sleep and mood records, journal notes and settings. Uninstalling the app, or Settings › Backup and restore › Erase all data, deletes them.

What the app reads, only with your permission and only on the phone

  • SMS: bank and payment messages, to log spends. Only the amount, payee and time are kept. Other messages are ignored, and no message ever leaves the phone.
  • Microphone: listens on the phone for the wake phrase. Voice commands are turned into text by your phone's speech service (from Google or your phone's maker), under their privacy policy. Offline mode makes the phone use its offline speech pack.
  • Location: only while you track a run. The route is saved on the phone.
  • Camera: only to scan a barcode. No photo is saved.
  • Usage access: when the screen turned on and off, to estimate your sleep. No other app data is read.
  • Fingerprint or screen lock: checked by Android. Dinam never receives your fingerprint.

What can leave your phone, only when you use it

  • Packaged-food search and barcodes: the words you search or the barcode number are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), under its privacy policy. We add nothing that identifies you.
  • "Search how to do it" links: open a web search in your browser.
  • Backups: a backup file goes only where you choose to save or send it. Dinam's data is never sent to cloud backup. Android's direct phone-to-phone transfer, when you move to a new phone, can copy it across.
  • Offline mode (Settings › Privacy and lock) turns off every internet feature.

What we never do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data, and no artificial intelligence services.

Age

Dinam is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to give back or delete. You control everything in the app. For any question or complaint, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, write to support@dinam.co.in. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date, and the app tells you about important changes.
